## Ringing Song

**Role: Project Lead & Lead UX/Prototype Designer**

I led concept development and team coordination for a four-person project in **Pillar 3: The User Experience (Dashboard & Visualization Design)** of the **2025 Sustainability Expertise Case Competition**, sponsored by CPRBS. Our project redesigned the existing Gies Sustainability Dashboard around the needs of university leaders, students, and donors. I conceived most of the persona journeys and built and iterated on most of the interactive website prototype using Figma Make. I also authored the Overview and Blueprint sections of the team's report and presentation.
