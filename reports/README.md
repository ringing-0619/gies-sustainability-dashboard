## Data clarification

**All data in the dashboard is simulated.** This clarification was provided by the project lead when preparing this portfolio repository and applies to the prototype, screenshots, and figures reproduced in these documents.

The report's assumption of access to complete institutional data is a design premise. Descriptions such as "historical data," "real-world outcomes," "proven success stories," "live updates," and projected funding impacts should be read as prototype content, not as verified evidence.

Publication and faculty records, institutional comparisons, donor information, funding figures, success stories, policy outcomes, economic impacts, matching funds, tax examples, and simulator outputs are illustrative. No empirical institutional findings or measured usability improvements are established by these artifacts.

The repository's [case study](../docs/case-study.md) provides the current framing. The original PDFs have not been rewritten.
