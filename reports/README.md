# Original team documents

- [Team report](Group5_report.pdf): 46 pages.
- [Team presentation](Group5_ppt.pdf): 49 pages.

Both documents are preserved as supplied and credited to **Linyao Gao, Ringing Song, You (Mia) Wu, and Samyak Pokharna**.

They document the team's entry in **Pillar 3: The User Experience (Dashboard & Visualization Design)** of the **2025 Sustainability Expertise Case Competition**, sponsored by the Center for Professional Responsibility in Business and Society (CPRBS).

The report and presentation explain the team's dashboard redesign, seven-module structure, visualization choices, and journeys for leaders, students, and donors.

## Data clarification

**All data in the dashboard is simulated.** This applies to the prototype, screenshots, and figures in these documents.

The simulated content supports the interface's publication metrics, faculty profiles, institutional comparisons, donor information, funding figures, success stories, policy and economic impact examples, matching funds, tax examples, and simulator outputs.

The [design case study](../docs/case-study.md) presents the team's design process, user journeys, and visualization gallery.
